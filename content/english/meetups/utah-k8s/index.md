Utah Kubernetes is where local engineers gather to talk about running containers and cloud-native systems in the real world. Expect practical talks on cluster operations, networking, observability, GitOps, security, and the broader CNCF landscape — from people actually running these systems in production.

Whether you're evaluating Kubernetes, running it at scale, or just trying to understand the ecosystem, you'll find approachable talks and engineers happy to compare notes.
