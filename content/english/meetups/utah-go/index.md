The Utah Go User Group brings together Go developers from across the Wasatch Front for regular meetups. Sessions range from beginner-friendly introductions to deep dives on concurrency, tooling, performance, and real-world Go systems in production.

Whether you write Go every day or are just curious about the language, you'll find a welcoming community, practical talks, and people happy to help you level up. Most talks are recorded and posted to the [Forge Utah YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/ForgeUtah).
