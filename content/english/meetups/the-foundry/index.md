The Foundry Hack Nights are Forge's recurring open work sessions. Bring a side project, a thing you've been meaning to learn, or just yourself — it's unstructured time to build alongside other technologists, get unstuck, and meet people in the community.

No agenda, no slides, no pressure. Show up, plug in, and make something.
