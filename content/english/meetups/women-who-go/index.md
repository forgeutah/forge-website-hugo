Women Who Go Utah is the local chapter of the global Women Who Go network, dedicated to supporting women and non-binary developers working with the Go programming language. The group hosts talks, workshops, and social events in an inclusive, welcoming environment.

Everyone is welcome — whether you're new to Go or a seasoned Gopher looking to connect with a supportive community.
