The Utah Data Engineering Meetup (UDEM) brings together the people who build and operate data platforms across the state. Talks cover ingestion and ETL, warehouses and lakehouses, streaming, orchestration, data quality, and the practical trade-offs of building reliable data systems.

It's a great place to learn what other teams are running, swap war stories, and keep up with a fast-moving corner of the industry.
