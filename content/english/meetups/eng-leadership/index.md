Engineering Leadership Utah is a gathering for the people who lead engineering teams — managers, tech leads, directors, and VPs. Conversations focus on the parts of the job that aren't in the code: growing people, setting technical direction, navigating org change, hiring, and the day-to-day realities of leadership.

Come to trade hard-won lessons with peers who understand the role and are working through the same challenges.
