AI Utah is where local machine learning and AI practitioners meet to share what they're building and learning. Talks span applied machine learning, large language models, MLOps, computer vision, and the research and tooling shaping the field.

Whether you ship ML in production, experiment on the side, or are just getting started, it's a welcoming place to learn from people working across the spectrum of AI.
